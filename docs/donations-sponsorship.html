Donations & Sponsorship

Support the society and one another by investing in student growth, engineering education, innovation, and practical opportunities that strengthen our community.

Student support

Help students access the resources and opportunities they need to succeed academically and socially.

Innovation

Back engineering projects, prototypes, and competitions that turn ideas into meaningful solutions.

Industry growth

Partner with ICES to connect students with professional practice, mentorship, and technical exposure.

1. Introduction

ICES welcomes contributions and partnerships that improve student experiences, strengthen engineering education, promote innovation, and build a more connected and capable society. Support can take many forms, from a small personal gift to a meaningful industry partnership, and every contribution can make a real difference.

We are grateful to students, fellow society members, alumni, families, individuals, and organisations who believe in creating opportunity, sharing knowledge, and investing in the next generation of engineers.

2. Student-to-Student Support

We encourage fellow students to support one another in ways that are practical, respectful, and rooted in solidarity. Students may contribute according to their means, and meaningful support does not always require large amounts of money. Contributions can include:

  • Voluntary financial contributions.
  • Textbooks, revision materials, and other useful learning resources.
  • Engineering drawing instruments and other academic materials.
  • Calculators and other useful educational equipment.
  • Support for fellow students participating in approved academic or society activities.
  • Other forms of assistance coordinated through the society.

We approach this support with dignity and confidentiality. Any programme involving financial assistance to individual students should be coordinated transparently, with appropriate confidentiality and authorisation, so that support remains fair, respectful, and aligned with approved society processes.

3. Individual Donations

We invite alumni, staff, individuals, and other supporters to contribute towards approved society activities that benefit the wider student community. Donations may support:

  • Engineering projects and innovation competitions.
  • Technical visits and educational workshops.
  • Student development activities.
  • Learning resources and academic support.
  • Society events and approved operational needs.

4. Corporate Sponsorship and Industry Partnerships

We welcome partnerships with engineering companies, construction firms, consultants, contractors, suppliers, and other organisations that share our commitment to learning, innovation, and professional excellence. Sponsorship and partnership opportunities can include:

  • Sponsoring engineering events and exhibitions.
  • Supporting student innovation projects.
  • Donating equipment and learning materials.
  • Supporting technical visits and professional development.
  • Offering mentorship, industry exposure, or other relevant in-kind assistance.
  • Establishing longer-term partnerships with the society.

We are pleased to discuss sponsorship arrangements with organisations interested in investing in the future of engineering talent and practice. If your organisation would like to explore a partnership, please get in touch with the society.

5. Transparency and Accountability

Donations and sponsorships should be authorised, properly recorded, and used for their stated purposes. ICES will ensure that contributions are managed transparently and that support is aligned with the approved activities and goals of the society. We do not make guarantees or claim tax benefits, and we do not publish or invent official financial policies, audited accounts, or partnerships that have not been formally approved.

6. How to Contribute

At present, our donation and sponsorship enquiries are handled through direct contact with the society. Online payment processing is not currently available on this site, so interested supporters are invited to get in touch by email or phone to discuss the most appropriate form of support.
